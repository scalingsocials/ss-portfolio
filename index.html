How we buy media

Four rules every account runs on, whether it spends ₹40,000 or ₹5 lakh a month.

Rule 1

Your margin sets the target

We work out your break-even ROAS from gross margin, shipping and returns. Campaigns that clear it get budget. The rest get switched off.

Rule 2

Creative is made here

You send raw footage and product shots. Our designers and editors turn them into statics and video ads, with new angles tested every week.

Rule 3

Your money, your accounts

Ad spend goes directly to Meta and Google. No markup, and you own every account, pixel and audience.

Rule 4

We show the worst month

Every case study in this deck names its soft month. A floor you can plan around beats a peak you can't repeat.
